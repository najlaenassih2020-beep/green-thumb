      Green Thumb

Find your perfect plant and bring nature into your space!
Green Thumb is a plant-shopping web application built with React. It allows users to explore and shop for different plants, discover new favorites, and take an interactive quiz that helps them find the perfect plants based on their preferences and lifestyle.

     Features

Plant Shop: Browse and explore a variety of plants.

 Interactive Quiz: Answer a few questions to discover plants that match your preferences.

 Personalized Recommendations: Find plants suited to your lifestyle and needs.

 Modern UI: Enjoy a clean and user-friendly shopping experience.

    🛠️ Built With

React.js

JavaScript

HTML5

CSS3
